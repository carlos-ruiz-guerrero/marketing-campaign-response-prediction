# Executive Summary

## Business Objective

The goal of this analysis was to identify customer characteristics and campaign factors associated with successful term deposit subscriptions in a bank marketing campaign.

## Key Findings

### 1\. Older Customers Represent the Most Valuable Segment

Customers aged 60 and above achieved a conversion rate of 42.3%, substantially outperforming all other age groups. This segment appears to be the most receptive audience for term deposit marketing campaigns.

### 2\. Higher Account Balances Are Associated with Greater Conversion Rates

Customers with very high account balances achieved a conversion rate of 16.2%, compared with 7.2% for customers in the lowest balance segment.

### 3\. Campaign Fatigue Strongly Reduces Performance

The highest conversion rate was observed after a single contact (14.6%). Conversion rates progressively declined as the number of contact attempts increased, reaching only 5.8% for customers contacted six or more times.

### 4\. Previous Customer Engagement Improves Outcomes

Customers who had been previously contacted converted at a rate of 23.1%, compared with 9.2% for customers with no prior contact history.

### 5\. Distinct High\-Value Customer Profiles Exist

The most successful customer profiles were concentrated among customers aged 60\+ across several professions, including self\-employed, entrepreneur, management, and retired segments.

## Preliminary Recommendations

1. Prioritize marketing investments toward customers aged 60\+.
2. Focus campaigns on higher\-balance customer segments.
3. Reduce excessive contact attempts to avoid campaign fatigue.
4. Implement targeted retention and re\-engagement campaigns for previously contacted customers.
5. Develop specialized offers tailored to high\-performing customer personas.

## Next Steps

The next phase of the project will evaluate whether these observed relationships are statistically significant and identify the most influential predictors of customer subscription behaviour.
